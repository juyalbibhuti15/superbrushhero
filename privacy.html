Privacy Policy

Application: Super Brush Kids

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Super Brush Kids is designed for families and children. We take privacy seriously and design the app to collect and transmit as little information as reasonably necessary to provide the app and manage subscriptions.

Privacy at a glance
  • No account or login is required.
  • No third-party advertising is displayed.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use child information for targeted advertising.
  • Child profiles and brushing history are designed to remain on the device.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and subscription payments.
  • RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status and purchase restoration.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Super Brush Kids allows a parent or guardian to create child profiles and track brushing activity.

Information stored locally on the device may include:

  • A child's nickname or first name entered by the parent or guardian.
  • Profile or avatar preferences.
  • Brushing session activity.
  • Stars, streaks, rewards, and progress information.
  • App settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide the app's brushing routines, progress tracking, and reward features.

Super Brush Kids does not intentionally send child profile names or brushing-history information to RevenueCat.

2. Information We Do Not Require From Children

Super Brush Kids does not require a child to create an account or provide:

  • An email address.
  • A phone number.
  • A home address.
  • A precise geographic location.
  • Contacts.
  • Payment-card information.
  • Social-media information.

Parents and guardians should use a nickname or first name only when creating a child profile and should not enter sensitive personal information.

3. Subscription and Purchase Information

Purchases and subscriptions for the iOS version of Super Brush Kids are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your complete payment-card number, bank-account information, or Apple Account password.

Super Brush Kids uses RevenueCat to help determine whether a subscription is active, validate purchase status, and support Restore Purchases.

RevenueCat may process limited information necessary to provide these services, such as:

  • An App User ID, which may be anonymously generated.
  • Subscription and purchase transaction information.
  • Subscription product identifiers.
  • Subscription status and expiration information.
  • App Store transaction or receipt information.
  • Device and operating-system information necessary to provide the service.
  • Limited technical information used to operate and protect the service.

This information is used for subscription management, entitlement verification, purchase restoration, fraud prevention, and related app functionality.

4. Advertising

Super Brush Kids does not display third-party advertising.

We do not use child information for behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, or advertising profiles.

5. How Information Is Used

Information associated with Super Brush Kids is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide child profiles and brushing routines.
  • Track brushing progress, stars, streaks, and rewards on the device.
  • Provide subscription-based access.
  • Validate and restore App Store purchases.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect purchase and subscription systems from misuse or fraud.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Sharing With Service Providers

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Limited information may be processed by service providers required to operate purchases and subscriptions, including:

  • Apple — App distribution, App Store transactions, subscription billing, and payment processing.
  • RevenueCat — Subscription status, purchase validation, entitlement management, and Restore Purchases functionality.

These providers process information according to their own applicable privacy policies, contractual obligations, and security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Super Brush Kids is designed for children to use with the involvement and supervision of a parent or guardian.

We intentionally minimize the information associated with children. Children are not required to create an account, provide contact information, or provide payment information.

Super Brush Kids does not use children's information for targeted advertising.

Purchases and subscription decisions should be made by a parent or guardian through Apple's purchase system.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us through a support request or another communication channel, please contact us so we can review and appropriately address the request.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Child profiles, brushing history, stars, streaks, and related progress information are designed to be stored locally on the device.

Removing the app may remove locally stored information from the device.

Subscription and transaction records processed by Apple or RevenueCat may be retained as necessary to validate subscriptions, restore purchases, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, maintain appropriate transaction records, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Because Super Brush Kids does not require an account, we may not possess information that directly identifies a particular app user.

For a privacy or deletion request, please contact us using the email address below. We will assist with information under our control where reasonably possible.

9. Your Choices

You may stop using Super Brush Kids at any time.

You may remove locally stored information by deleting profiles where that functionality is available or by deleting the app from the device.

App deletion does not cancel an active App Store subscription. Subscriptions must be managed separately through your Apple Account subscription settings.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information associated with Super Brush Kids. However, no electronic-storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keeping child-related profile and progress information on the user's device reduces the amount of child information transmitted to external systems.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app, our service providers, or applicable legal requirements.

When the Privacy Policy is updated, the effective date displayed at the top of this page will also be updated.
