Subscription Help

Super Brush Kids subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store are billed and managed by Apple.

How to cancel your subscription

On your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name / Apple Account at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Super Brush Kids.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and follow Apple's instructions.
Important: Deleting Super Brush Kids from your device does not cancel an active Apple subscription.

Free trial

If you started an eligible free trial, you can cancel through your Apple subscription settings before the trial ends to avoid the next subscription charge. Trial eligibility and billing are determined by Apple.

Restore Purchases

If you previously subscribed and reinstall the app, use the Restore Purchases option inside Super Brush Kids.

Purchases must be associated with the same Apple Account that was originally used for the subscription.

Refunds and billing questions

Payments made through the App Store are processed by Apple. Apple controls billing, subscription charges, refund eligibility, and payment-method issues.
