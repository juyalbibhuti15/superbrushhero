Subscription Help

Super Brush Kids subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store are billed and managed by Apple.

How to cancel your subscription

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name / Apple Account.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Super Brush Kids.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and follow Apple's instructions.
Important: Deleting Super Brush Kids from your device does not cancel an active subscription.

Free Trial

Super Brush Kids may provide eligible customers with an introductory free trial. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple.

Unless canceled before the applicable trial ends, the subscription automatically converts to the paid subscription shown when the purchase was started.

Restore Purchases

If you previously subscribed and reinstall the app, use the Restore Purchases option available on the Super Brush Kids subscription screen.

Restoration generally requires the same Apple Account that was used for the original purchase.

Refunds and billing

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. Apple manages subscription billing, payment methods, and refund eligibility.
